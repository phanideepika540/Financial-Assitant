# Mutual Funds

A mutual fund pools money from investors and invests according to the fund's
stated objectives and strategy. An investor buys shares in the fund rather
than directly owning each security held by it. Funds differ in the assets
they hold, their risks, management approach, and costs.

Pooling does not guarantee diversification or a positive return. Review the
fund's current prospectus, holdings, objectives, and fees before drawing
conclusions about its role in a portfolio. Past performance is not a
guarantee of future results.
