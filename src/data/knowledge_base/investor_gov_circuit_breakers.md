# Market Volatility Procedures and Circuit Breakers

Markets may have procedures intended to address extreme price movements or
disruptions. Circuit breakers can pause or constrain trading under defined
conditions, but they do not prevent losses or ensure that trading resumes at
a particular price.

The Investor.gov glossary page points readers toward explanations of these
procedures. Operational thresholds and exchange rules can change; consult
current exchange and regulator materials rather than relying on a static
summary during a fast-moving market event.
