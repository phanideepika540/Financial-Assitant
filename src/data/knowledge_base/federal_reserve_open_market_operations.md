# Open Market Operations

Open market operations are purchases and sales of securities by a central
bank as part of implementing monetary policy. The Federal Reserve explains
how these operations relate to the policy objectives set by the FOMC and
the supply of reserve balances in the banking system.

This background can help decode a policy headline, but it does not predict
the direction of interest rates, asset prices, or economic activity.
Implementation details have evolved over time. Refer to the Federal
Reserve's current materials and clearly distinguish operational facts from
market commentary or speculation.
