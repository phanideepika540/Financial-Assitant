# Tax Topic no. 503, Deductible Taxes

IRS Tax Topic 503 describes categories of state, local, and foreign taxes
that may be relevant to an individual's federal itemized deductions. The
deductible amount and treatment can depend on the type of tax, payment,
taxpayer circumstances, and current law.

Limits and reporting instructions are especially sensitive to the tax year.
Use the current IRS topic and Schedule A instructions rather than relying
on a past return or a general summary. This article does not determine
whether a particular payment is deductible.
