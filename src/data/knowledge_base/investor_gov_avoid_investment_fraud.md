# What You Can Do to Avoid Investment Fraud

Investment claims deserve independent checking, especially when they
promise high returns with little or no risk, create pressure to act quickly,
or rely on an unfamiliar person or organization. Looking up the investment
and the people offering it through official sources can help identify
questions that need answers before money is sent.

Fraud tactics change, and a polished website or confident explanation is
not proof that an offer is legitimate. Avoid relying on testimonials or
social-media claims as verification. Consult current SEC alerts and report
suspicious offers through official channels.
