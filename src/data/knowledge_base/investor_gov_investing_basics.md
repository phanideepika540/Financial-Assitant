# Introduction to investing

Investing involves putting money into assets with the possibility of growth
and the risk of loss. Stocks represent ownership in a company; bonds are loans
to an issuer. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Before making an investment decision, learn how the investment works, its
risks and costs, and whether its time horizon and possible losses fit the
purpose for which the money is being saved. A longer time horizon does not
remove risk. Consult current official investor education materials for
definitions and product-specific details.
