# Tax Topic no. 502, Medical and Dental Expenses

IRS Tax Topic 502 explains when certain medical and dental expenses may
qualify for a federal itemized deduction and points to the relevant
reporting rules. Whether an expense qualifies can depend on the type of
expense, who incurred it, reimbursement, and the rules for the tax year.

This is not a calculation of an individual's deduction. Thresholds,
definitions, and forms can change. Check the current-year IRS topic and
instructions before claiming an expense, and keep records supporting any
amount reported.
