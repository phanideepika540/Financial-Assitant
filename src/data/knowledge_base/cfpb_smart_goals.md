# Setting SMART goals

The CFPB worksheet helps turn a broad financial priority into a goal that is
specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound. Writing down what
success would look like, how progress will be measured, and when the goal
should be revisited can make next steps easier to identify.

A goal should reflect the person's circumstances and may need to change when
income, expenses, or priorities change. The worksheet is a planning aid, not
a guarantee that a target is affordable. This individual handout dates to
2018; check the CFPB page for current materials and adapt the exercise to
current circumstances.
