# Market Participants

Securities markets include individual and institutional investors,
broker-dealers, exchanges, and other participants with different roles.
Understanding who may be involved in a transaction helps explain how orders
reach a market and why trade execution is not simply a direct exchange
between two individual investors.

The SEC oversees U.S. securities markets and works with other regulators.
This overview is not a complete description of every market structure or
rule. Regulatory responsibilities and market practices can change, so use
current agency materials for specific questions.
