# Savings plan

The CFPB worksheet helps a person define a savings goal, choose an amount to
set aside, and consider a contribution schedule. Breaking a large target into
smaller steps can make progress easier to track and can reveal whether the
timeline or contribution amount needs adjustment.

The result depends on the person's inputs and does not account for every
possible change in expenses, income, or savings returns. Treat it as a
planning exercise rather than a promise about future account balances. This
individual handout dates to 2018; consult the CFPB for current materials.
