# Cash Flow Budget Tool

The CFPB tool organizes income and expenses by the dates money arrives and
bills are due. Looking at timing as well as monthly totals can help identify
periods when available cash may be tight and when a bill date or savings
transfer may need attention.

The worksheet supports planning; it does not determine whether a particular
expense should be paid first or replace a person's own review of obligations.
Use current figures, include irregular expenses where possible, and revisit
the plan when income or bill timing changes. This individual handout dates to
2018; check the CFPB page for current versions.
