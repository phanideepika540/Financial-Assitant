# Bank account basics

The CFPB's bank-account resource provides an entry point for consumers
learning about checking and savings accounts and related account questions.
When comparing accounts, a person can review the provider's current fee
schedule, access options, balance requirements, and account terms rather
than assuming that all accounts work the same way.

This source is a starting point, not an endorsement of a financial
institution. Product terms and consumer protections can change. Follow the
CFPB links and confirm details with the bank or credit union before opening
an account.
