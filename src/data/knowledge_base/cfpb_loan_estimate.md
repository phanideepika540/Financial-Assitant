# Loan Estimate Explainer

A mortgage Loan Estimate summarizes important proposed loan terms and
estimated costs. The CFPB explainer helps readers locate key entries, check
that the document reflects what they discussed with a lender, and compare
estimates from different lenders.

Comparisons should use the current documents and the same assumptions where
possible. A Loan Estimate is not a final closing statement, and costs or loan
terms may change under applicable rules and circumstances. Ask the lender to
explain discrepancies or terms that are unclear; use the CFPB's current
interactive guide for the form-specific details.
