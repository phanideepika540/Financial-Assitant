# About U.S. Savings Bonds

TreasuryDirect describes Series EE and Series I savings bonds, including how
they are purchased and held. Savings bonds have specific terms for interest,
redemption, and tax treatment; those details differ by series and issue date.

They are not the same as a bank savings account and may have restrictions on
when they can be redeemed. Rates, purchase limits, and terms can change, so
check TreasuryDirect for the current rules that apply to a particular bond
before comparing it with other ways to save toward a goal.
