# How do I dispute an error on my credit report?

The CFPB advises consumers who find a possible report error to explain what
they believe is wrong and provide supporting information to the relevant
credit reporting company. A person may also contact the company that supplied
the information. Keeping copies of reports, letters, and delivery records
can help document the process.

The exact steps depend on the item and current procedures. Use the CFPB's
current instructions and sample materials, send copies rather than original
documents, and avoid sending sensitive information through unverified
channels. This overview is educational and does not decide whether a
particular entry is inaccurate.
