# Daily Treasury Par Yield Curve Rates

The Treasury publishes par yield curve rates across a range of maturities.
The curve is a snapshot of rates on Treasury securities and can help readers
compare yields across time horizons. It is a market data series, not a
forecast of future rates or a complete explanation of why yields changed.

Rates move over time and may be revised or displayed with different
conventions. When discussing a current yield, identify the observation date
and maturity, and consult the Treasury's latest data. A Treasury yield is
not the return guaranteed on every bond investment.
