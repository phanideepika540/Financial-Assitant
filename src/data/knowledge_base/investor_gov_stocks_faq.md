# Stocks - FAQs

A stock generally represents an ownership interest in a company. Share
prices can move as expectations, company information, and broader market
conditions change. Owning stock can involve both the possibility of gains
and the risk of losing money.

This Investor.gov overview introduces stock ownership; it does not provide
current prices or a view about a particular company. When assessing a
company, use its current public disclosures and distinguish reported facts
from forecasts, opinions, and market commentary.
