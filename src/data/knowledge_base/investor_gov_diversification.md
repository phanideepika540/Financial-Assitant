# Diversification

Diversification means spreading investments across different assets so that
one holding has less influence on the overall portfolio. It does not
eliminate market risk or guarantee a profit.

A mutual fund or exchange-traded fund can hold many investments, but a fund
can itself be concentrated. To understand possible concentration or overlap,
review the fund's current holdings and investment objectives rather than
assuming that a fund name reveals its full exposure.
