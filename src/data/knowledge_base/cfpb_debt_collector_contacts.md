# What should I do when a debt collector contacts me?

The CFPB recommends learning enough about a claimed debt to assess whether
the collector and debt are legitimate. Consumers can review the information
provided about the creditor, amount, and ways to dispute the debt, and should
avoid sharing sensitive financial information until they have verified who
is contacting them.

Debt-collection rights and deadlines depend on current law and a person's
circumstances. Use the CFPB's up-to-date guidance for validation, disputes,
and response options. If a legal deadline or lawsuit is involved, consider
getting qualified legal help rather than relying on a general educational
summary.
