# Deposit Insurance

FDIC deposit insurance protects eligible deposits at FDIC-insured banks if
an insured bank fails. Coverage depends on the ownership category, depositor,
and institution; it does not apply to every financial product or to every
company that uses the word "bank."

Consumers can use the FDIC's BankFind tool to verify whether an institution
is insured and review current coverage information. Do not assume that
investment products, crypto assets, or balances held outside an insured
deposit account are protected by FDIC insurance. Confirm current rules
directly with the FDIC.
