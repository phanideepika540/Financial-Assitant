# IRS Tax Topics

The IRS Tax Topics collection is a starting point for general federal tax
information across subjects. A topic page is not a substitute for checking
the current year's forms, instructions, publications, and rules that apply to
a taxpayer's situation.

When a question depends on a tax year, identify the year before relying on
limits or rules. Do not treat this index as complete guidance for an
individual tax outcome.
