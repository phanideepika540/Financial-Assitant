# Asset Allocation and Diversification

Asset allocation describes how a portfolio is divided among broad types of
investments. Diversification spreads exposure within and across those
categories, while rebalancing adjusts a portfolio when its mix changes over
time. These are different concepts that can work together to manage
concentration and risk.

There is no allocation that is right for everyone. Goals, time horizon, and
ability to tolerate losses differ, and diversification cannot prevent all
losses. Use current fund and account information to understand what a
portfolio actually holds; this overview does not provide an individualized
allocation.
