# Money Smart for Older Adults Program

The FDIC and CFPB curriculum is designed to help older adults and caregivers
recognize and reduce the risk of financial exploitation. Its materials
cover awareness, advance planning, and ways to make more informed financial
decisions. The curriculum can be used as a whole or by individual topics.

This source is an educational program, not a way to report an urgent crime or
recover funds. Check the FDIC page for the latest instructor guide, resource
guide, and presentation materials; scam tactics and contact information can
change.
