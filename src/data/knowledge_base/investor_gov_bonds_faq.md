# Bonds - FAQs

A bond is a debt security: an issuer borrows from investors and generally
promises interest payments and repayment of principal under stated terms.
Bonds vary by issuer, maturity, credit risk, payment structure, and market
price. A bond's value can change before it matures.

The word "bond" does not mean that an investment is risk-free. Investors
should understand the issuer's ability to pay, how interest-rate changes can
affect market value, and the costs of buying or selling. Use current offering
documents and disclosures for a specific bond.
