# Gross Domestic Product (GDP)

The Bureau of Economic Analysis publishes estimates of U.S. gross domestic
product, a broad measure of economic activity. GDP releases summarize
changes in output and contributions from areas such as consumer spending,
investment, government activity, and trade.

GDP estimates are released in stages and can be revised as more information
becomes available. A headline growth rate is not a complete account of
household well-being or a prediction of financial-market performance. When
discussing a release, identify the quarter, estimate vintage, and source,
and distinguish reported data from interpretation.
