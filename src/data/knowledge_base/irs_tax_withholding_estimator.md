# Tax Withholding Estimator

The IRS estimator helps individuals review federal income-tax withholding
using information about income, household circumstances, deductions, and
credits. A person may find it useful after a major life or income change, or
when a paycheck or pension withholding amount no longer seems aligned with
their circumstances.

The tool's output depends on the inputs and tax-year rules. It is not a tax
return or individualized professional advice. Use the current estimator for
the relevant year, protect the personal information entered into it, and
review withholding again when circumstances change.
