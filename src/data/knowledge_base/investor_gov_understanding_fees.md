# Understanding Fees

Investment fees and expenses reduce the portion of investment returns that
remains for an investor. Charges can arise from a fund, transaction, account,
or service, and they may be described in different documents. Comparing
costs alongside services and investment features can help make trade-offs
visible.

The actual charges depend on the product and provider. Use current account
documents and a fund's prospectus or fee table rather than relying on an
old example or a headline expense ratio alone. This general overview does
not estimate any person's costs.
