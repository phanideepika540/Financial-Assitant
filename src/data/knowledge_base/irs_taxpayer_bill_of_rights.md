# Taxpayer Bill of Rights

The IRS summarizes fundamental rights taxpayers have when dealing with
the federal tax system, including the right to be informed, to receive
quality service, to challenge the IRS's position, and to appeal certain
decisions. These principles describe how tax administration should work;
they do not guarantee a particular outcome in an individual case.

Deadlines and remedies depend on the notice, procedure, and current law.
Read the specific IRS communication carefully and use current IRS guidance
or qualified assistance when responding to a tax matter.
