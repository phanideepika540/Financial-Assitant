# Understanding fees and expenses

Investment fees and expenses reduce the amount of an investment's returns
that remain for the investor. Costs may include ongoing fund expenses,
transaction charges, or charges for services.

Compare costs and services using current product disclosures. For a fund,
review its current prospectus and fee table rather than relying only on a
summary. The effect of costs depends on the amount invested, time, and
applicable fees; do not infer a user's actual costs without the relevant
documents.
