# Filing

The IRS filing hub links to federal tax information for individuals,
businesses, and other taxpayers. Whether a person must file and which forms
apply depend on the relevant tax year and individual circumstances. Filing
requirements are not determined solely by whether tax was withheld from
pay.

Use the IRS resources for the specific year and situation, and check current
forms, deadlines, and instructions. This article is a navigation aid rather
than a determination that a particular person must file or qualifies for a
specific treatment.
