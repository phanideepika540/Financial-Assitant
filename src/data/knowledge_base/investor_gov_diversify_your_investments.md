# Diversify Your Investments

Diversification means spreading investments so that one security or category
does not determine the entire portfolio's outcome. It can reduce the effect
of a loss in one holding, but it cannot prevent losses across a market or
guarantee a gain.

A portfolio can appear diversified while still holding overlapping
investments or concentrated exposures. Review underlying holdings and
investment objectives rather than relying only on the number of funds or
their names. This concept is distinct from choosing an allocation that fits
a person's circumstances.
