# Individual Retirement Arrangements (IRAs)

Traditional and Roth IRAs generally differ in the tax treatment of
contributions and qualified distributions. Eligibility, contribution limits,
deductibility, and distribution rules can depend on the tax year and a
person's circumstances.

Do not infer eligibility or calculate a user's tax outcome from a general
question. Consult the current IRS IRA guidance for the relevant tax year and
seek qualified tax help for individual circumstances.
