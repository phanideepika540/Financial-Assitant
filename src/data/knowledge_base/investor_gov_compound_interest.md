# Compound Interest Calculator

The Investor.gov calculator lets users explore how an initial amount and
assumed contributions or growth rate may affect a hypothetical future
balance. Compounding means that returns may themselves generate returns
over time, so the path depends on the assumptions entered.

Calculator output is an illustration, not a prediction or guarantee.
Investment returns are uncertain, and taxes, fees, inflation, and changes in
contributions can affect actual results. Compare scenarios without treating
an assumed rate as an expected or assured return.
