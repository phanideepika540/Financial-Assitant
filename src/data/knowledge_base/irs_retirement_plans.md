# Retirement plans

Employer-sponsored retirement plans and individual retirement arrangements
are subject to plan-specific and tax rules. Eligibility, contributions,
distributions, and tax treatment depend on the type of plan, tax year, and
individual circumstances.

An account type is separate from the investments held inside it. Use current
IRS publications and the plan's documents for current rules, and consult a
qualified tax professional for questions about personal circumstances.
