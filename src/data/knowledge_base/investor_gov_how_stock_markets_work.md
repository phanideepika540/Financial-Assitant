# How Stock Markets Work

Stock markets bring together buyers and sellers through exchanges and other
trading venues. Prices can change as orders interact and as participants
respond to information, expectations, and available liquidity. A quoted
price is not necessarily the price at which every order will be executed.

Market structure and trading practices can evolve. Use this resource for
basic concepts rather than current market conditions, and consult a
broker's current order-handling information when considering how a specific
trade may be processed.
