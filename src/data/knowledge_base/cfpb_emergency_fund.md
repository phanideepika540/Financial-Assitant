# An essential guide to building an emergency fund

An emergency fund is money set aside for unplanned expenses, such as a car or
home repair, medical bill, or loss of income. The CFPB explains that the
appropriate amount depends on a person's situation; looking at unexpected
expenses experienced in the past can help them choose an initial savings
target. Even a small amount may provide some financial security when the
ability to save is limited.

Possible strategies include creating a consistent savings habit, tracking
cash flow, saving some of a one-time inflow, or arranging automatic transfers.
Automatic transfers should be balanced with monitoring account balances to
avoid possible overdraft fees. A person can define what they consider an
emergency, use the reserve when needed, and work to rebuild it afterward.

The guide discusses keeping emergency money somewhere safe and accessible.
Options and trade-offs depend on the person; the guide does not prescribe one
provider or product.
