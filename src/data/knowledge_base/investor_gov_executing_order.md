# Executing an Order

The price and overall result of a trade can depend on how and where a
broker routes and executes an order. Execution may differ from the displayed
quote, particularly when prices move or trading conditions change. Brokers
may use different venues or methods subject to applicable rules.

This SEC overview explains execution concepts; it is not a comparison of
individual brokers. Review current order-routing disclosures, fees, and
broker terms to understand the practices that apply to a particular account.
