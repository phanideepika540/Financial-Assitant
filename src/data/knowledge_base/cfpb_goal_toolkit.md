# Your Money, Your Goals toolkit

The CFPB toolkit organizes financial empowerment resources around setting
goals, savings, tracking income and bills, cash-flow budgeting, and dealing
with debt. Its goal-setting tools encourage people to describe a goal and
consider actions, large purchases, life events, and revisions as circumstances
change.

A planning conversation can use a cash-flow view to compare when money comes
in with when bills and expenses are due. This can reveal timing shortfalls
that a monthly total alone may hide. Debt logs and action-planning tools can
help a person collect information and consider options; the toolkit does not
establish one universal order for every person's financial priorities.

The toolkit page lists individual handouts and describes the complete toolkit
as updated June 2020. Check the CFPB page for current availability and
materials.
