# Checklist for opening a new bank or credit union account

The CFPB checklist helps consumers prepare questions and information before
opening a checking or savings account. Comparing account terms in advance
can help a person understand how to access funds and what requirements or
charges may apply.

Use the checklist alongside current disclosures from the institution. A
worksheet cannot confirm that an account is suitable for a particular
person, and account terms may change. Ask the provider to explain any fees,
conditions, or services that are unclear before opening the account.
