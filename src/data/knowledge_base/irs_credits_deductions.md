# Credits and deductions for individuals

Tax deductions generally reduce the amount of income subject to tax, while
tax credits generally reduce tax owed. Eligibility and the way an item is
claimed depend on the applicable law, the taxpayer's facts, and the tax
year.

The IRS page links to information about individual credits and deductions.
Do not assume that a credit or deduction applies based only on its name or
a prior year's rules. Review current IRS guidance and forms, and seek
qualified tax help when eligibility or reporting is unclear.
