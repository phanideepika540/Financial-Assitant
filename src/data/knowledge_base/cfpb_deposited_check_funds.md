# Learn when your funds from a deposited check will be available

The CFPB explains that a bank or credit union may place a hold on a deposited
check before making funds available. Availability timing can depend on the
deposit and account circumstances, while federal rules establish limits on
how long certain holds may last.

The date a deposit appears in an account is not always the date all funds
can be spent. Check the institution's current account agreement and the
CFPB's current explanation when planning around a deposit; do not rely on a
general estimate for a time-sensitive payment.
