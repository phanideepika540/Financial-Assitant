# Find out your financial well-being

The CFPB's ten-question tool measures a person's self-reported financial
well-being and offers steps they may consider to improve it. It is intended
as a reflection and planning aid, not as a credit score, financial diagnosis,
or measure of whether someone has made good choices.

The tool's questions do not request personal financial account data. A
conversation can use the results to clarify what financial security or
flexibility means to the person and identify a manageable next step. The
interactive tool requires a supported browser; check the source page for
current access and instructions.
