# About the Federal Open Market Committee

The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) is responsible for key U.S.
monetary-policy decisions, including open market operations. Its statements,
minutes, and press conferences are primary sources for what the Committee
announced and how participants described economic conditions.

News coverage may interpret a decision differently from the official
statement, and market expectations can change before an announcement.
Compare commentary with the current FOMC materials, note the release date,
and separate the Committee's stated rationale from forecasts or claims
about future market moves.
