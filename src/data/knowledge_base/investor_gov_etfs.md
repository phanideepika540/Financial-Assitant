# Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs)

An exchange-traded fund pools investor money into a portfolio and issues
shares that trade on an exchange. ETF structure, investment objective,
holdings, trading activity, and costs can vary from one fund to another.

An ETF's exchange-traded format does not remove investment risk or ensure
that it is low-cost or broadly diversified. Review the current prospectus,
holdings, and fee information, and understand how trading prices may relate
to the fund's underlying value. This is general product education, not a
recommendation to buy or sell a fund.
