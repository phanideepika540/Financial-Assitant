# How do I get a free copy of my credit reports?

The CFPB explains how consumers can request and review credit reports from
the nationwide credit reporting companies and points to the authorized
request service. Reviewing reports can help a person spot information they
do not recognize or believe is inaccurate.

Credit reports are different from credit scores, and the information in a
report can differ across reporting companies. Access procedures and
availability can change, so follow the CFPB's current instructions and use
the official request channel linked there. Do not share credentials or
identity information through links in unsolicited messages.
