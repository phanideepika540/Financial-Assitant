# National Strategy for Financial Literacy (2026)

The Financial Literacy and Education Commission's 2026 strategy describes
federal priorities for financial education, including saving, investing, and
awareness of scams. It provides policy context about public education
efforts; it is not a consumer-specific financial plan or a source of
individual product recommendations.

Use the strategy to identify broad educational themes and follow its links
to the relevant agencies for practical guidance. Its recommendations describe
the 2026 strategy period and should not be treated as a timeless statement of
law or as a substitute for current agency rules.
