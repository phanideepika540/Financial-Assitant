# Monetary Policy

The Federal Reserve describes U.S. monetary policy as actions intended to
promote maximum employment, stable prices, and moderate long-term interest
rates. Policy decisions and communications can affect financial conditions,
but their effects on markets and the economy are not immediate or
mechanical.

This page links to current Federal Open Market Committee materials. When
interpreting a policy headline, distinguish the announced decision from
market expectations and from later economic outcomes. Use the latest
Federal Reserve statements for current decisions; this overview is not
investment advice.
