# Topic No. 409: Capital Gains and Losses

Selling an asset for more or less than its adjusted basis may result in a
capital gain or loss. Tax treatment can depend on the asset, holding period,
account, tax year, and the taxpayer's full circumstances.

Keep transaction records and use current IRS instructions for the relevant
tax year. This educational summary does not determine a taxpayer's liability;
consult a qualified tax professional about personal tax questions.
