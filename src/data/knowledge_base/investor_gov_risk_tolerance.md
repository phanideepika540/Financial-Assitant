# Gauge Your Risk Tolerance

Risk tolerance concerns how a person responds to the possibility of losing
some or all of an investment. The time before money is needed and the
purpose of that money are also relevant when thinking about investment risk.
A person's comfort with uncertainty is only one part of understanding risk.

No questionnaire or short conversation can determine a suitable portfolio
for every individual. Investments can lose value, including over periods
when a person may need the money. Use this Investor.gov resource to frame
questions, not as a personalized risk assessment or forecast.
